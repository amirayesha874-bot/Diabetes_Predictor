Understanding diabetes

Educational resources on risk factors, prevention and healthy living.

🔎 Key risk factors

  • Overweight / obesity – especially fat around the waist.
  • Family history – a parent or sibling with type 2 diabetes.
  • Age – risk increases after 45.
  • High blood pressure (≥ 130/80 mmHg) and abnormal cholesterol.
  • Physical inactivity and smoking.
  • Pre-diabetes – fasting glucose 100–125 mg/dL.
  • Gestational diabetes or multiple pregnancies.

🛡️ Prevention strategies

  • Lose 5–7% of body weight if overweight – cuts risk by up to ~58% in trials.
  • Exercise 150 minutes per week (e.g. brisk walking).
  • Choose whole grains, vegetables, legumes, lean protein; limit sugary drinks.
  • Don't smoke; limit alcohol.
  • Get screened regularly if you have risk factors.

🥗 Healthy-lifestyle guidelines

  • Fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables.
  • Prefer water, unsweetened tea; avoid fizzy/sweetened drinks.
  • Sleep 7–8 hours; chronic sleep loss worsens insulin resistance.
  • Manage stress – walking, prayer/meditation, hobbies.
  • Reduce salt to help control blood pressure.

🚨 Warning signs – see a doctor

  • Increased thirst and frequent urination
  • Unexplained weight loss, constant hunger
  • Blurred vision, slow-healing wounds
  • Tingling in hands or feet, extreme fatigue

ℹ️ About the risk categories

Low < 35% Moderate 35–65% High ≥ 65%

Categories are derived from the model's predicted probability of diabetes. They indicate screening priority, not a diagnosis.
