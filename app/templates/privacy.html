Privacy policy

What we collect

Your name, email, role, and the health readings you enter (age, height, weight, glucose, blood pressure, lifestyle answers) together with the resulting predictions.

How it is used

Only to generate your risk prediction, recommendations, history and reports, and to let your assigned healthcare professional review your results. If you submit a confirmed outcome as feedback, those readings may be used to retrain the model.

How it is protected

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Health inputs, recommendations and clinician notes are encrypted at rest. Sessions expire after 15 minutes of inactivity and accounts are locked for 10 minutes after 5 failed logins. Use HTTPS when deploying on a server.

Sharing

We never sell or share your data with third parties. Only you, your assigned healthcare professional and system administrators (for support) can access your records.

Your rights

You can edit your profile, delete individual predictions, export your data (PDF/CSV) and permanently delete your account and all associated data from Profile → Delete account (right to erasure).

Medical disclaimer

This system supports clinical decision-making and does not replace professional medical judgment.
